# Candlestick Pattern Reference

Quick-reference catalog for the day-trading-advisor skill. Patterns are grouped by type and ranked by reliability.

## Reversal Patterns (Bearish to Bullish)

| Pattern | Candles | Description | Reliability |
|---------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Hammer** | 1 | Small body at top, long lower wick (2x+ body), little/no upper wick. Appears at bottom of downtrend. | High |
| **Inverted Hammer** | 1 | Small body at bottom, long upper wick. Appears at bottom of downtrend. Needs bullish confirmation next candle. | Medium |
| **Bullish Engulfing** | 2 | Bearish candle followed by larger bullish candle that fully engulfs the prior body. | High |
| **Morning Star** | 3 | Large bearish candle → small-bodied candle (gap down) → large bullish candle closing into the first candle's body. | High |
| **Piercing Line** | 2 | Bearish candle → bullish candle that opens below the prior low and closes above the midpoint of the prior body. | Medium |
| **Three White Soldiers** | 3 | Three consecutive bullish candles with progressively higher closes. Each opens within the prior body. | High |
| **Tweezer Bottom** | 2 | Two candles with matching lows at the bottom of a downtrend. Second candle is bullish. | Medium |

## Reversal Patterns (Bullish to Bearish)

| Pattern | Candles | Description | Reliability |
|---------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Hanging Man** | 1 | Same shape as hammer but at the top of an uptrend. Signals potential reversal. | Medium |
| **Shooting Star** | 1 | Small body at bottom, long upper wick. Appears at top of uptrend. | High |
| **Bearish Engulfing** | 2 | Bullish candle followed by larger bearish candle that fully engulfs the prior body. | High |
| **Evening Star** | 3 | Large bullish candle → small-bodied candle (gap up) → large bearish candle closing into the first candle's body. | High |
| **Dark Cloud Cover** | 2 | Bullish candle → bearish candle that opens above the prior high and closes below the midpoint of the prior body. | Medium |
| **Three Black Crows** | 3 | Three consecutive bearish candles with progressively lower closes. Each opens within the prior body. | High |
| **Tweezer Top** | 2 | Two candles with matching highs at the top of an uptrend. Second candle is bearish. | Medium |

## Indecision / Neutral Patterns

| Pattern | Candles | Description | Action |
|---------|---------|-------------|--------|
| **Doji** | 1 | Open and close are nearly equal. Long wicks = high indecision. | Wait for confirmation |
| **Long-legged Doji** | 1 | Doji with very long upper and lower wicks. Extreme indecision. | Wait for confirmation |
| **Dragonfly Doji** | 1 | Doji with long lower wick, no upper wick. Bullish if at support. | Mildly bullish at support |
| **Gravestone Doji** | 1 | Doji with long upper wick, no lower wick. Bearish if at resistance. | Mildly bearish at resistance |
| **Spinning Top** | 1 | Small body with upper and lower wicks of similar length. | Wait for confirmation |

## Continuation Patterns

| Pattern | Candles | Description | Reliability |
|---------|---------|-------------|-------------|
| **Rising Three Methods** | 5 | Large bullish → 3 small bearish candles within the first body → large bullish. Continuation of uptrend. | High |
| **Falling Three Methods** | 5 | Large bearish → 3 small bullish candles within the first body → large bearish. Continuation of downtrend. | High |
| **Bullish Marubozu** | 1 | Full bullish body with no wicks. Strong buying pressure. | High |
| **Bearish Marubozu** | 1 | Full bearish body with no wicks. Strong selling pressure. | High |

## Pattern Confirmation Rules

1. **Volume matters**: Patterns are more reliable when accompanied by above-average volume
2. **Trend context**: Reversal patterns only apply when there IS an established trend to reverse
3. **Support/resistance**: Patterns at key S/R levels are significantly more reliable
4. **Timeframe**: Patterns on higher timeframes (1h, 1d) are more reliable than lower (1m, 5m)
5. **Never trade a single pattern in isolation** — always combine with indicators (RSI, MA, MACD)
